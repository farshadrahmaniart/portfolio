02 — FEATURED CASE

NEXA20.COM — EMS modern fitness technology

NEXA20.COM   UX/UIFrom scratch19 pages2025Figma + Photoshop + Illustrator

Open live site ↗
Role
Product Designer (UI/UX) — research, IA, wireframes, UI, handoff and QA.
Brief
Booking-focused hero with Book Now flow. Responsive marketing site for mobile and desktop. Explain EMS simply, present services, drive booking with trainer and time selection.
Users
Athletes and tech-curious users who want maximum results in minimum time in sports.
What I did
Benchmark, sitemap and IA, wireframes, foundations and components, desktop and mobile UI, dev-ready delivery.
Key decision
Three-step booking with persistent CTAs. Concise service presentation, trainer selection and high usability. Dark high-energy hero, clean form contrast.
Outcome
Live at NEXA20.COM. Key strengths: service clarity, booking logic and on-brand language.
